About Fleet Safety Notes

Independent, plain-English guides for people who run small UK fleets.

Who writes this site

I'm Zain, and I'm based in London. From 2021 to 2025 I worked in data annotation and quality assurance for a fleet-safety technology company. Day to day, that meant reviewing, cleaning and validating large volumes of dash cam video, images and telematics data, and labelling the footage used to train AI dash cam models that detect risky driving.

That work gave me an unusual view of fleet safety: not the sales pitch, but the raw data underneath it. I saw what the cameras capture well, where AI alerts go wrong, and how much the quality of the data decides whether a safety system actually helps drivers.

Before that I spent several years in customer-facing roles in the telecoms and technology sector, including selling fleet management products, so I also know the questions fleet owners ask before they buy. I hold a BSc in Computer Science and am studying for an MSc in Business Analytics at the University of East London.

What you'll find here

  • Buying guides that explain trade-offs honestly, without vendor spin
  • Explainers on how fleet data and AI scoring actually work
  • Free calculators and tools for everyday fleet decisions
