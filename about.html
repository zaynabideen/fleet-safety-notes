About Fleet Safety Notes

An independent publication with practical, plain-English guidance for people who run vehicles for a living.

Our mission

Running a fleet means making expensive decisions about safety technology, fuel, compliance and drivers, often with little time and a lot of sales pressure. Fleet Safety Notes exists to make those decisions easier. We explain how fleet technology works, what the evidence and the rules actually say, and which questions to ask before you commit, so you can decide for yourself.

Who we write for

  • Owners of small and mid-sized businesses that run vans, cars or trucks
  • Fleet, transport and operations managers
  • Anyone responsible for driver safety, vehicle costs or compliance in the UK

What we cover

  • Fleet safety: driver behaviour, risk scoring, coaching and incident handling
  • Dash cams and video telematics: camera types, AI alerts, installation and data protection
  • Telematics and vehicle technology: tracking, diagnostics and the data behind them
  • Fuel efficiency and running costs: practical ways to cut spend
  • Fleet management: compliance, policies and day-to-day operations

Alongside our guides we build free calculators and tools for everyday fleet decisions.

Independence

Fleet Safety Notes is not owned by, or affiliated with, any dash cam, telematics or vehicle manufacturer. Brand names are used only to describe products and remain the property of their owners. The site is funded by advertising and, where clearly disclosed, affiliate links. Commercial relationships never decide what we recommend. Read our editorial standards for how we research, review and fund our content.
