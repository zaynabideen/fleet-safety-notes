Privacy policy and disclaimer

How this site uses cookies and advertising, and what your rights are. Last updated November 2026.

Who is responsible for your data

Fleet Safety Notes is run by Zain Ul Abideen in London, United Kingdom. You can contact me about privacy at hello@fleetsafetynotes.co.uk.

What data is collected

  • Emails you send me. If you email, I keep your message and address only to reply and handle your request.
  • Calculator inputs. The fuel calculator remembers your last inputs in your own browser (local storage). They are never sent to me.
  • Server logs. The hosting provider may log standard technical data such as IP address and browser type for security.
  • Analytics. If analytics is used, it measures pages visited and general location so I can see which guides are useful.

Advertising and cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to show adverts.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also learn more at How Google uses cookies in advertising.
  • Visitors in the UK and EEA are asked for consent before personalised ads or non-essential cookies are used, through a consent message shown on your first visit. You can change your choice at any time.

Legal basis and your rights

Under UK GDPR, I process emails on the basis of legitimate interest (replying to you) and advertising cookies on the basis of your consent. You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, email me. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Retention

Emails are deleted once they are no longer needed, and in any case within 24 months.

Disclaimer

The content on this site is general information for fleet operators. It is not legal, insurance or financial advice, and laws and products change. Always check current official guidance (for example from DVSA and the ICO) and take professional advice before making decisions. Calculator results are estimates based on the figures you enter. Every effort is made to keep information accurate, but no responsibility is accepted for decisions made based on it.
