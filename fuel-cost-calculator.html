How the calculation works

  1. Weekly fleet miles = vehicles × miles per vehicle per week.
  2. Gallons used = miles ÷ mpg. UK mpg uses the imperial gallon, which is 4.546 litres.
  3. Litres = gallons × 4.546. Cost = litres × price per litre.
  4. Yearly figures assume 52 weeks; monthly figures are the yearly total ÷ 12.
  5. The saving compares today's cost with the cost at the improved mpg (mpg × (1 + improvement)).

A note on mpg: US mpg figures use the smaller US gallon (3.785 litres), so a US figure will look lower than the UK figure for the same vehicle. Make sure you enter UK mpg.

Where fuel savings come from

Harsh acceleration and braking, long idling and high motorway speeds all waste fuel. These are the same behaviours a driver risk score tracks, which is why safety coaching and fuel savings usually go together.
