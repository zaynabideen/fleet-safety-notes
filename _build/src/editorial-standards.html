Editorial standards

How we research, write, check and fund the content on Fleet Safety Notes.

Accuracy and sources

Our guides are researched from primary sources wherever possible: government and regulator guidance (such as FMCSA in the US, DVSA and the ICO in the UK, and their equivalents elsewhere), legislation, and manufacturers' own documentation. Each article lists the main sources it relies on, so you can check them yourself.

Where rules or products vary, we say so. Where something is general information rather than advice for your situation, we say that too.

Review and updates

Every article shows the date it was last reviewed. We revisit guides regularly, and sooner when laws, official guidance or products change.

Corrections

If we get something wrong, we fix it promptly and, for significant errors, note the correction on the article. Please tell us if you spot a mistake.

Use of AI tools

We may use AI tools to help with research, outlining and drafting. AI is never the final word: every article is fact-checked against its sources and edited by the Fleet Safety Notes editorial team before publication, and we take full responsibility for what we publish.

Independence, advertising and affiliate links

  • Advertising. The site shows adverts, including through Google AdSense. Advertisers have no influence over our content, and adverts are kept visually separate from articles.
  • Affiliate links. Some articles may include affiliate links. If you buy through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Articles that contain affiliate links say so clearly near the top.
  • No paid reviews. We do not accept payment for reviews or for favourable coverage. Products are never ranked based on commission.

Not professional advice

Our content is general information for fleet operators. It is not legal, insurance or financial advice. For decisions about your own business, check current official guidance and take professional advice. See our full disclaimer.
