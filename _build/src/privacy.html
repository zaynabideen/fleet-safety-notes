Privacy policy and disclaimer

How this site uses cookies and advertising, and what your rights are. Last updated November 2026.

Who is responsible for your data

This website is published by the operator of Fleet Safety Notes ("we", "us"), based in the United Kingdom, who is the data controller for personal data collected through this site. You can contact us about privacy at hello@fleetsafetynotes.co.uk.

What data we collect

  • Emails you send us. If you email us, we keep your message and address only to reply and handle your request.
  • Calculator inputs. Our calculators remember your last inputs in your own browser (local storage). They are never sent to us.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider may log standard technical data such as IP address and browser type for security.
  • Analytics. If we use analytics, it measures pages visited and approximate location so we can see which guides are useful.

Advertising and cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to show adverts.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also learn more at How Google uses cookies in advertising.
  • Visitors in the UK, EEA and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads or non-essential cookies are used, through a consent message shown on the first visit. You can change your choice at any time.

Affiliate links

Some pages contain affiliate links. If you click one, the retailer or partner may set a cookie so it can recognise that you came from this site. See our editorial standards for how we handle affiliate relationships.

Legal basis and your rights

Under the UK GDPR, which applies to us, we process emails on the basis of legitimate interest (replying to you) and advertising cookies on the basis of your consent. You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, email us. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). If you live outside the UK, you may have additional rights under your local privacy laws; contact us to exercise them.

Retention

Emails are deleted once they are no longer needed, and in any case within 24 months.
