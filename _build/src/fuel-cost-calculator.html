How the calculation works

  1. Weekly fleet distance = vehicles × distance per vehicle per week (miles are converted to km at 1.609 km per mile where needed).
  2. Fuel used: UK mpg uses 4.546 litres per gallon, US mpg uses 3.785 litres per gallon, and L/100km is used directly.
  3. Cost = fuel used × price. Prices per US gallon are converted at 3.785 litres per gallon.
  4. Yearly figures assume 52 weeks; monthly figures are the yearly total ÷ 12.
  5. The saving compares today's cost with the cost at an improved fuel economy (fuel use ÷ (1 + improvement)).

Watch out for mixed units: the same vehicle shows a lower figure in US mpg than in UK mpg because the US gallon is smaller.

Where fuel savings come from

Harsh acceleration and braking, long idling and high motorway or highway speeds all waste fuel. These are the same behaviours a driver risk score and a telematics system track, which is why safety coaching and fuel savings usually go together.
